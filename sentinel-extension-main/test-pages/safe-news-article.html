Police warn of rise in gift card scams

Local police say residents have reported a jump in calls from people pretending to be from the IRS or Microsoft support.

"No real government agency or tech company will ever ask you to pay with gift cards," said Sgt. Dana Kim. Scammers often claim your computer is infected or that you owe back taxes.

If a pop-up tells you to call a phone number, close the browser instead. Report scams to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
